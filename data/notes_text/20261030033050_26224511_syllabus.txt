COURSE OVERVIEW
         Electric Vehicle System Engineering program at REVA University, a uniquely industry-driven course, fully immersing
         students in hands-on learning experiences. With an academic MATLAB license (No. 41185143) at their disposal,
         students will undertake initial simulations in our cutting-edge Simulation Lab, designed to provide a practical
         foundation in electric vehicle systems. What sets our program apart is the collaboration with Bosch Pvt Ltd, where
         industry experts will actively engage with our students, bridging the divide between academia and real-world
         application. These experts will not on




           enrich the learning experience but also guide students in successfully completing their course assignments, ensuring
           that they are well-prepared for the challenges and opportunities in the electric vehicle industry.


COURSE OBJECTIVE


               1.    To present a comprehensive overview of Electric Vehicles
               2.    To enable students to know the characteristics of various types of energy storage devices .
               3.    To present a comprehensive overview of Power Electronics and Electric Drive Train Systems.
               4.    To equip students with basic concepts to practically design/ implement and modify the existing vehicle to

COURSE CONTENTS THEORY



Unit 1: Introduction to Electric Vehicles
              Introduction to EVs, Configuration of EVs, Performance of EVs, Energy Flow Analysis, Introduction to Traction
              Batteries. Drive cycle and its importance in system analysis. Component selection for a given drive cycle for a
              BEV. Three Phase Inverter, VSI, PWM Techniques- Space Vector PWM- Hysteresis Control, Comparison of PWM
              techniques
Module 2: Energy Storage System

             Ragone plot, Battery Types and battery pack, Basic battery operation, Battery
      Chemistry -Li Ion, Electric Vehicle Battery Efficiency and Effects on Performance,
      Calculation of Electric Vehicle Battery Capacity, SoC and SoH estimation,
      Governing equations and Waveforms, Battery parameters and comparisons, BEV –
      battery sizing, Modelling of Battery
            -RC equivalent network Model, Concept of cell balancing, Basic battery
      management systems. Battery Chargers- Basic requirements for charging
      systems, Classification of Charging Architectures, Charging Controls, Current
      Regulations, charging standards and technologies.


Unit 3: Introduction to Power Electronics and Electric Drive Train Systems
            Power Electronic Converters for Electric and Hybrid Vehicles, Electric
      Vehicle Charger Topologies, Electric Drive train Overview, Systems with Linear
      Motion and Rotating Systems, Types of loads, Four Quadrant Operation, Induction
      Motor for EV power train - Steady State Analysis of Induction Drive, Direct & Indirect
      Vector Control, Field Oriented Control, Machine Model with Leakage Inductance.
      Permanent magnet synchronous machine for EV power train, Non-Salient & Salient
      Drives, Generic Model, Steady State Analysis, Field Oriented Control.




                Unit 4: EV Modelling & Simulation

            Modeling of BEV-Forward looking Model-Driver Perspective, Backward
      Looking Model Drive Cycle Perspective, Modelling of Driver, Modeling of Brake
      Control Unit/Regenerative braking , Modeling of Vehicle Control Strategy, Modeling
      of Vehicle Chassis Sizing of Components- Steady State Energy Balance Equation,
      Modeling of Battery/Cell, Powertrain Dimensioning Peak vs Continuous
      performance, Type of Drive cycles, Types of Control Strategy, Analysis
      Performance, Range, Consumption Prediction.
